Remix the Internet: LevelUp Vibe Hackathon Dares Coders to Reinvent Their Favorite Websites

May 2, 2025 3 min read

Outlier AI has kicked off the LevelUp: Vibe Coding Hackathon, a fast-paced global coding sprint where developers are challenged to rebuild and improve iconic websites — adding creative features, smarter workflows, or bold UX enhancements.

Participants can choose any existing site, replicate its layout and structure, then "level it up" with an original twist. Whether it's turning a static blog into a personalized content hub or adding AI-driven recommendations to a news platform, originality is the name of the game.

With entries due by Sunday, May 4 at 11:59 PM PT, competitors are racing against time to build something both familiar and forward-thinking. Judging criteria include usability, innovation, and whether the updated version genuinely improves upon the original.

Submission Requirements:

  • A live hosted link (Vercel, Netlify, etc.)
  • GitHub repository
  • A short description of enhancements
  • The name of the reference site

Top entries could win major prizes, including a MacBook Pro, PlayStation 5, and AirPods Pro Max — plus freelance opportunities and partner perks for standout projects.

Developers can attend live office hours on May 3 at 3:30 PM IST for support and feedback.

"Make it smart, make it bold — and make it unmistakably you."

— Outlier AI
